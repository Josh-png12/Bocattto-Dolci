## Direction contract

THESIS: The kraft cup seen from above is the stage. Every dessert is shown as what it is in life — a circle of cream inside a kraft rim, sealed with Bocatto's round carmine sticker. Refuses the restaurant template (split hero + three equal cards + menu grid).

OWN-WORLD: Peach #FCECDF ground with kraft (#D8BE97) fields; carmine #9E122C drenches whole regions (explorer on berries, quiz, footer); orange #EE6A43 for discounts and the striped card border; cacao #2A1712 for type and the chocolate chapters. Fraunces Black SOFT 100 / WONK headlines, DM Sans UI. Components: round sticker seals, kraft tickets, the striped "tarjeta personalizada" card, menu rows with dotted leaders.

STORY: I see Bocatto's real cheesecake and price immediately → I play with flavors → I learn what's inside (base, crema, mermelada) → the quiz finds my craving → I tap "Agregar", pick the add-on and quantity, review my order in the cart, and send it by WhatsApp for Bocatto to confirm. No redirect to OlaClick; sending is not a confirmed purchase.
